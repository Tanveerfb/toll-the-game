Meliodas's real card text, as the archive renders it. Today the game colours keywords cyan, numbers white and footnote terms sky blue, which all work on a dark panel and fail as text on paper. Each option below takes a different position on what replaces colour. Tap a dotted or marked keyword to open its hint.

What is fixed in every option: skill-class chips and the ultimate's five-hue frame stay as fills (they already are), page and section headers are the skewed paper labels, and the text itself is M PLUS 1p at 14px. What changes is how a keyword, a number and a footnote term stand out, which today is colour: text-signal cyan for keywords, sky-300 for footnote terms, emerald-400 for heal amounts, rose-300 for Uncancellable.
