## Why this exists

The 3–6 draft was written against what I'd read at the time. The source folder
actually holds **twelve** chapters — 7 through 12 are all marked LOCKED
(approved 2026-08-05) and none of them had been mapped. They are also, by some
distance, the most gameplay-dense stretch of the story so far: Phase 2 of the
Grading, the Molvarr encounter the game already ships as a boss, and the first
canon fight between two playable characters.

Three of the six need decisions before any of them can be authored. Those are
at the bottom, not buried in the tables.

## Part 7 — "Other Tables" (webtoon Ch7)

Base difficulty 2. Trial level 18.

**Zero Duke and Lyra.** The entire chapter is Chiara in an admin room
recounting three Phase 1 duels she examined, shown as desaturated flashbacks.
It introduces Batra, Gabrist, Mustafa, Siddiq, Sara and Yalina in one go.

| Ch | Title | Beats | Battle | Team mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7-1 | The Gambler's Table | Admin room, Chiara's entrance, the framing | — scene only | — |
| 7-2 | Pain and Patience | Recollection: Batra takes a hit and gets stronger; Gabrist unravels a technique and pays for it | Batra + Gabrist vs Chiara | `canon` |
| 7-3 | Nothing to Bet On | Recollection: Mustafa's wall, Siddiq's vines, no offense to punish | Mustafa + Siddiq vs Chiara — **the fight with no finish** | `canon` |
| 7-4 | Mouth and Legs | Recollection: Yalina baits, Sara bleeds her out, one committed strike lands | Sara + Yalina vs Chiara | `canon` |

**This is the strongest argument yet for playing someone other than Duke.** All
three are two-versus-one, all three are already choreographed in the source,
and all six characters are playable kits that exist today. The player would
take the *pair* in each; Chiara is the enemy.

**7-3 is the interesting problem.** The source is explicit that it has no
finish — Chiara throws everything and there is nothing to win against, so she
stops. A standard "reduce the enemy to zero" battle would contradict the beat
outright. Options: a survive-N-turns objective (see the engine gap below), or
cut it to scene-only, which is now supported.

**Fidelity note the source volunteers:** the escalation beats deliberately
shadow Molvarr's real kit without naming a skill, and the corrosion-transfer
beat is the lifesteal made visible. Whatever gets built should keep that — the
mechanic is the storytelling.

## Battles you aren't meant to win — RULED 2026-08-11

Three of these are not "reduce the enemy to zero":

| Battle | What winning means |
| --- | --- |
| 7-3 Mustafa + Siddiq vs Chiara | Nobody wins. Chiara gives up. |
| 8-2 Duke vs Batra | Both land something; neither goes down. |
| 9-1→9-3 Molvarr | Survive and cross. He's still there at the end. |

**Tanveer's ruling: the fights still happen, and they end early.** When the
enemy drops to **20% HP** the battle auto-ends as a victory, the scene panels
explain what actually happened, and rewards pay out normally. The panels carry
the narrative weight — Chiara conceding, the two of them breaking off, the
crossing — rather than the battle pretending to resolve it.

That's `victoryAtEnemyHpPercent` on a chapter's battle: **built 2026-08-11**,
see `lib/game/victoryCondition.ts`. Absent means the old rule (kill everything),
so every existing chapter is untouched.

It suits Molvarr better than a turn limit would. His self-heal and his one-time
stat doubling stay fully in play — they're what makes 20% hard to reach and
hold, which is exactly the fight the source describes.

## Yalina — RULED 2026-08-11

Yalina is a **playable character with a shipped kit**, she is **featured on the
live gacha banner**, and chapter 10 kills her.

**Tanveer's ruling: she stays playable, and the story doesn't reconcile it.**
Precedent cited: Himeko in Honkai Impact 3rd. Characters die in canon and keep
playable versions; the two layers don't owe each other consistency. No banner
rotation, no walk-back, nothing to build.

7-4 uses her as a playable lead in Chiara's recollection, which is set before
her death anyway — so the order the game tells this in never puts a living
Yalina after a dead one.

## Seras on screen — RULED 2026-08-11

**"Seras" and "Seris" are the same character** (Tanveer, 2026-08-11 — the two
spellings appear interchangeably in the source and in these notes). She is the
playable Light unit `data/characters/seras.json`, she has portrait and skill
art, and she is featured on the live banner. Worth stating plainly here because
the two spellings are one typo apart and reading them as two people would put a
villain with no assets into the plan alongside a playable with a full kit.

**Tanveer's ruling: conceal her, don't invent a second character.** Drop the
name and credit the line to **"Unknown female voice"**, and/or show her darkened
rather than in her combat portrait.

**She already has two arts** (Tanveer, 2026-08-11):

| Form | File | Registered? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combat | `public/characters/seras.png` | Yes — `characterArt.ts` |
| Civilian | `public/unreleased/seras_civilian_wip.png` | **No** — WIP, unregistered |

So the concealed appearances don't need a black rectangle invented for them —
they want the **civilian form**, which is exactly what the Ch10 after-credits
calls for: *"Her civilian form, first glimpse ever offered of it, and still
nothing given away."* Silhouetting is then a CSS treatment over real art rather
than a separate asset.

The unnamed-speaker half is a **data choice per scene, not a code change** — the
renderer takes a speaker name per line.

**Small engine note, not a blocker:** `characterArt.ts` maps one art per
character id, with no concept of an alternate form. `public/unreleased/` already
holds four more — `chiara_alt-dealing`, `isolde_alt-serene` and two
`sea_monster` variants — so whenever alternates are wanted on screen, that
registry needs a second axis. Nothing here depends on it; the scene could just
reference the file directly.

This settles the clash flagged at the top of the 3–6 draft. Part 6 keeps its
"intrigue only" framing with an unnamed speaker, and chapter 10 is where the
name lands — a stronger reveal than naming her four parts early, and it costs
nothing to author that way.

## Part 7's cast — RULED 2026-08-11

**Trial versions of those six, against Chiara as the enemy.** That's the
existing lent-unit path (`storyTrialIds` / `resolveStoryTeam`) doing exactly
what it was built for: the pair are `canon` anchors, and anyone the player
doesn't own is lent at the part's trial level for that battle only.

So part 7 needs no new mechanic and no ownership gate — a player who owns none
of the six still plays all three fights.
