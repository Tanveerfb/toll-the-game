Part 1 — how a mid-run fight ends

Three positions on one question: is a fight an event, or a step?

A fight is a step, not an event. The victory card is deleted for any fight that is not the last one — the win lands you straight on the road, with the node you just cleared flipped to Cleared and carrying its own one-line result. Nothing new to read; the screen you were going to see anyway now also answers "what just happened". 1 tap to continue

Costs you the beat. There is no moment of winning — the screen simply changes. On a three-fight run that may be right; on a boss it would not be.

After fight 1 — straight to the road

A fight is an event, and the card should earn its place. The VICTORY beat stays, but its body becomes a real summary — turns, HP lost, who fell, ultimates spent — and the button says what actually happens next. Then the road, unchanged. 2 taps to continue

This is the smallest change to what ships today and the only option that keeps a win feeling like a win. It is also the only one that asks a player to read two screens where one would do.

Screen 1 — the card (press NEXT FIGHT)
Screen 2 — the road

Both questions, one screen. The win is a banner at the top of the road rather than a card in front of it: what just happened and what is ahead are read in one movement, and the run's whole history accumulates down the page as each cleared node keeps its result. 1 tap to continue

Part 2 — how the run ends

Two positions on: is the payoff the unlock, or the run you just survived? Today this screen exists and reads Rank 20 cap lifted — accurate, and it never says congratulations or names what opened.

The reward is information about your account. Congratulate, then say plainly what changed, in the player's terms — the ceiling, the ranks that were banked against the wall and just paid out, the stamina refill. Short enough to read in one screen with nothing to scroll.

Trial complete — with recap
Ideas beyond what you asked for, since you said you were open to them — each is independent of the choice above:

1. The defeat screen needs the same pass. A lost trial currently offers RETRY BATTLE, which re-enters the whole run and charges stamina again, under a button that says BACK TO WORLD BOSS. It should say what it costs.

2. Name the fight you are about to enter. The road says "Fight 2 of 3" and shows a portrait; it never says Lyra. The name is the one thing a player repeats to themselves.

3. Show the pooled HP as a number you can act on. The road already computes a pooled percentage. Under the no-heal rule that figure is the whole decision — carry on or abandon — so it could lead rather than sit at the bottom.

4. A cleared trial should stop looking enterable. Not drawn here, and worth checking on the board: the First Trial is repeatable: false, so once the wall is down the card has nothing left to offer.
