The shell at 390

Two layout questions the 2026-09-01 browser audit turned up, neither of which is a size fix. Everything marked measured came off the live deploy at 390×844, not off reading the source. Per ruling #106 these are proposals, not edits — nothing here is built.

1 · The top bar

Measured: the route strip is 234px holding 332px — News and Profile never render at rest. The resource row is 226px holding 293px; Bureau Orders is the clipped one and the avatar's right edge lands at 405px. Two independent horizontal scrollers, side by side, inside one 390px bar, neither with a scroll affordance. And navLabel — short labels written in routes.ts for exactly this case — is hidden sm:inline, so it has never rendered on a phone.
