Growth modal — tabs, and three ways to spend a stack

Drawn at 390 px, the width that matters. Everything below is the same modal; the three columns differ only in how the Level tab lets you spend. Ascend and Ultimate are drawn once, at the bottom, because there is only one proposal for each.

The problem being solved. Levelling spends one manual per tap. xpToNext = 100 × level, so Lv 20 → 40 is 59,000 XP — 590 basic manuals, 148 advanced, or 59 premium. Every one of those is a separate press today.
A constraint the current modal never shows, and every option below does. A manual also costs coin: COIN_PER_XP = 2. So Lv 20 → 40 is 118,000 coin on top of the manuals. At the coin balances I have seen on screen, levelling is coin-gated long before it is manual-gated — and nothing in the current design says so. If that is wrong, it is an economy question rather than a layout one, and it is yours.

What is the same in all three. Tabs carry their own value, so the modal answers "where am I" before you open a tab. Every tab then has the same four slots in the same order — where you are · what it costs · what you get · one button — and the button says the blocker rather than the word "Ascend". That is the consistency and modularization half; one CostChip and one GrowthStep serve all three.
My pick, if you want one: A for the shape, with C's coin line. A is the fewest decisions on screen and the milestones match how the caps actually work. B is more satisfying to use but it hides "how far can I get" behind trial and error. C is the most honest and the most to build. The ladder-instead-of-slider on Ultimate I would take regardless — it removes a control rather than adding one.
Not decided here, and yours: which manuals an auto-solve spends. Cheapest-first burns your basics and hoards premiums; largest-first is fewer items but wastes overflow at the cap. It decides whether premium manuals feel precious, so it is a design call rather than a layout one.
