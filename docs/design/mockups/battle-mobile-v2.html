The battle screen at 390

Three things Tanveer called out after the first look at a live battle on a phone: the hand cannot show its own cards, the bar above it is doing too much with too many words, and the field's fitness for 4v4 was never checked. All three are measured below on the running game at 395×751, in a real 4v4, with getBoundingClientRect — not read off the CSS.

1 · The hand hides two of its eight cards

Cards are flex: 1 1 0 between a 56px floor and a 96px ceiling. The floor is the problem: eight cards at 56px, plus seven 4px gaps and 16px of padding, need 492px. The scroller has 370px. It scrolls, so nothing is lost — but at rest the player is looking at six of their eight options, and the two they cannot see are the two on the end they never scroll to.

Measured in a live 4v4 — hand of 8, container 370px, content 492px, 122px hidden, 2 cards fully off-screen. In the 3v3 before it: hand of 7, 62px hidden, 1 card off-screen.

Card height comes down too. 56×128 is a 1:2.3 card; 47×107 keeps that proportion. That returns 21px to the field, which is exactly what section 3 needs. The name line loses about two characters — press-and-hold already opens the card (ruling #118), so the full name was never living on the face of it anyway.

2 · The action bar — and the two slots you cannot see

This one is not only clunky. The three queued-action slots are 56px each inside a scroller that measures 83px: 97px of it is clipped, so two of your three queued actions are invisible. The turn's whole state is in that strip.

The rest is redundancy. ACTIONS ▪▪▪ 3 LEFT takes 124px to say what three empty slots already say. RESET and END TURN take 141px of text for two controls whose icons are unambiguous.

Measured: chip 124px · slot scroller 83px holding 180px · Reset 60px · End Turn 81px. Total demand 445px in a 371px bar.

  • A keeps one row and 44px on everything. Reset becomes the universal ↻; End Turn keeps a word because it is the one irreversible control in the fight and an icon alone would not earn that. The three slots become flex-1, so they are ~76px each and all three are visible at every hand size.
  • B puts the slots directly above the hand, because that is where cards go — the destination sits next to the source, and the drag gets shorter. Costs a second 44px row.
  • Dropped in both: the ACTIONS / N LEFT chip. Three slots that fill up as you commit already carry it, and aria-label can keep the count for a screen reader without spending 124px.

3 · Is the field tuned for 4v4?

It fits, and nothing overflows — but it was sized for three. Going to four takes 25% off every tile's width, and the tile spends that on the two things a player reads mid-turn: the portrait and the HP number.

Measured in a live battle, same viewport. 3v3: tile pitch 120px, HP bar 99px. 4v4: tile pitch 96px, HP bar 74px. Field height 383px in both — two rows, so each row gets ~190px whether it holds three tiles or four.

What changes, and why

  • The portrait goes 4:5, not 1:1. A square tile at 96px pitch wastes its width on background; a taller crop keeps the face at the same size while the tile narrows. This is the single biggest legibility win and costs nothing but a crop.
  • The 21px the hand gives back goes here, so the two rows are ~200px instead of ~190 and the portrait does not pay for the narrower tile twice.
  • The name drops to 11px and truncates honestly. "GON FREECSS" does not fit 74px at any size worth reading; press-and-hold already opens the unit (ruling #118).
The honest answer to "is it tuned for 4v4": no, it was tuned for 3v3 and 4v4 inherits it. Nothing breaks and nothing overflows — this is a legibility change, not a bug fix, which is why it is here and not already done.

Not proposed here

  • Merge is 38×20 on a hand card — under the 44px floor twice over. It is a fix, not a layout question, so it does not need a mockup; it needs a decision about whether the arm-merge affordance stays a button on the card face at 47px wide.
  • The interaction notice replaces the whole control row. While an auto-merge notice is up, Skip, Speed and Controls — and with them Log, Foe, Team and Exit — do not exist. Recoverable by dismissing, but the way out of a battle should not be behind a toast.
  • The auto-merge notice truncates at 305 of the 350px it wants.
