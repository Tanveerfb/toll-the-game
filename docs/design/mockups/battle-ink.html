Battle, in ink

Every other screen is on the new look. The arena is the one place where where the paper goes is a real question: paper is what you read, the ground is the page, and a battle is mostly things you glance at mid-turn. Three answers, each taking a different position. All three share one live fight: tap a card to queue it, tap a queued card or slot to take it back, hold a card or a unit to read it (#118), tap ⌖ to focus fire, then End. Switch options mid-turn; the fight carries over.
