**Who updates this:** any session that researches 7DSGC art with him. It is
the evidence behind `docs/design/SKILL_ART_PLAN.md`, not a rulebook. His rules
are quoted there; everything below is observation. Source: his Discord server,
channels #skills and #cut-in, walked through with him live over screenshots.

## Skill art

His rules (quoted in docs/design/SKILL_ART_PLAN.md):
- Skill background = class colour: attack red, attack-debuff purple, heal green, buff blue, stance yellow.
- Poses and shots free. Ultimates and passives: free colour, free pose.
- Character power shows as optional effects (particles, or carried in the move).

Recurring (observation, not rules):
- Back / over-the-shoulder views are common (Meliodas s2+ult, knight, Emilia s1, Priscilla s1, Gawain x3). The face is not required.
- Figure often near-white line art, so the class colour owns the card. NOT universal: Priscilla is fully coloured.
- Element/power travels with the move (beam, slash trail, crystal burst).
- Ultimates: cinematic camera (from behind, extreme close-up, vast scene).

| unit | pose | weapon / power | palette, accent |

| Rapier girl (blue hair) | standing, weight on one leg, tiptoe; free hand raised HIGH and wide open | rapier hanging low and loose, a long thin diagonal | light-blue hair, maroon jacket, silver line of the blade |
| Academy Hel (his favourite) | standing, one hand raised open by her shoulder, calm; figure CROPPED at the shins by the bottom edge | a huge red-and-silver mechanical staff held upright, a long silver pole on the diagonal behind her, plus her POWER as a huge effect: teal spectral flames erupting from both bottom corners (aura-like). A floating ghost companion in a matching cap | black and white uniform, red weapon details, purple horns; the teal flame is the dominant colour |

Constant across all eight: transparent background (a character layer), the
figure spanning the canvas (usually full body, but Hel is cropped at the
shins where her flames fill the bottom), and a gesture or expression that
says who they are. Varies: airborne or standing; a weapon as accessory
(Gawain), carried (Lancelot) or dominant (Skuld); power shown (Gowther) or
not shown (Gawain, Priscilla).

## Cut-ins (used for ultimates; adopting them is undecided)

A bust crop of the card-art figure, head to chest, with HARD straight crop
edges on the right and bottom, like a panel sliding in from the screen edge.
Emilia keeps the card pose; Priscilla and Gowther swap in a punched-up
expression and a hand thrust at the viewer. One render reused, cropped.
There is also a wide parchment banner with the full figure (a profile or
collection asset, not a battle one).
