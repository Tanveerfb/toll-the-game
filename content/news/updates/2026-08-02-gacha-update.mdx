The Summon system is here — two banners, a milestone-based pity bar that's
always working in your favor, and a new way to power up characters you
already own.

## Debut Banner

- 12 characters on one banner at launch: Duke, Lyra, Batra, Gabrist, Sara,
  Yalina, Mustafa, Siddiq, Master Tao, Seras, Chiara, and Isolde
- Single pull or an 11-pull discount
- Duke and Lyra are still free through their usual paths (starter / Chapter
  2) — pulling them here on top of that just powers them up

## Permanent Banner

- A second, always-available banner using its own currency (Permanent
  Banner Tickets)
- Currently empty — characters get added to it over time

## Milestone Pity

No more guessing when a pull is "due." A visible bar tracks your spend and
unlocks two guaranteed rewards:

- **300** — claim a random character from the entire released roster
- **600** — claim any character featured on the current banner, your pick

Both milestones can be claimed independently, and the bar loops — keep
pulling past 600 and you'll build toward another set of rewards.

## Dupes Power You Up

Pulling a character you already own is never wasted — it raises their
Ultimate Level (up to 6), a new axis of character strength on top of
leveling and ascension.

## Every Miss Still Pays Out

A non-featured pull always returns something: coin, leveling materials, or
one of four new specialty materials tied to a character's element.

## Also in This Update

- Save data is now more resilient — cloud saves go through the same
  safety checks as local saves, so your progress can't get out of sync
  between the two
