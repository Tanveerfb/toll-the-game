This is the largest update the game has had. The story goes from two parts to
twelve, character investment stops being decorative, cards finally move like
they weigh something, and most of what you look at on the way there has been
rebuilt. Read the parts that matter to you — the short version is:
**everything you have earned now counts for something.**

## Your Level Finally Matters

Levelling, ascension and ultimate ranks have been stored on your account since
the World Boss update and, until now, changed nothing. A fully invested
character fought exactly as hard as one pulled a minute ago.

That's fixed.

- **Levelling doubles a stat** across the full climb to Lv60
- **Ascension adds another full stat's worth** across its six bands, so a
  maxed character lands near **three times** its base numbers
- **Ultimate ranks** grow an ultimate by 60% of its own multiplier across six
  levels — a 500% ultimate reads 500 / 560 / 620 / 680 / 740 / 800

Every stat grows by the same proportion, so nothing about your team's internal
balance shifts — the tank doesn't quietly overtake the attacker.

Your character pages show this now. The archive's statlines carry **your** level
and ascension rather than the catalogue's base numbers, with the base printed
underneath whenever the two differ. The bars still measure against the roster,
because that comparison is about who a character *is*, not how much you've fed
them.

## Trial Characters Say So

Story chapters lend you characters you don't own. They always did — that's how
a new account plays Duke's story without pulling Duke — but nothing said so, and
an unowned character appearing in your team read as a bug.

Loaned units now carry a **Trial** badge and a line explaining they're yours for
that battle only. They arrive at the chapter's trial level, so early story
fights aren't fought with level-1 stats.

**If you own the character, your copy is used instead** — always. There's no
level at which the loaner is better than the one you've invested in.

## Account Rank and World Level

A new ladder sits above your characters.

- **Account rank** climbs to 60. Story chapters pay rank XP on first clear;
  the world boss pays on **every** clear, which is what actually moves the bar
- **Ranking up refills your stamina**
- **World level** is a difficulty dial. It opens at rank 20 and again at 40,
  and you can turn it **down** as well as up — a lower setting makes the fight
  easier and pays proportionally less

Ranks 20 and 40 are **walls**: XP keeps accruing but the rank holds until you
clear an ascension trial. Nothing is lost while you wait — the moment a wall
falls, every rank you earned behind it lands at once.

The trials themselves are listed on the events board but **aren't playable
yet**. They arrive in a later update.

## Three on the Field, One on the Bench

Every battle is now **3 on the field with 1 sub**, on both sides. This was
always the intended format; only the practice sandbox actually enforced it,
which is why story and world-boss fights had been fielding four.

Your sub keeps its passive active from the bench, can't be targeted, and steps
onto the field at the start of a turn after a teammate falls.

Subs no longer appear on the battlefield itself — they're in the **Team** panel
until they enter. The world boss was tuned against four attackers, so expect it
to run longer.

## One Team Picker, and Presets

Team selection was three different screens with three different behaviours.
There's one now, everywhere in the game.

- **Save up to 8 presets**, shared across every mode. Each chip shows its
  members' portraits, so you pick a team by recognising it
- **Your last team is remembered** — no more rebuilding it from scratch on
  every visit. It's saved when you actually launch a battle, not when you're
  still deciding
- A preset that can't fully load — a story chapter anchoring a slot, or a
  character you don't own — **leaves that slot open and tells you why**. It
  never silently swaps someone in, and never edits the saved preset

## The Whole Story

**Parts 3 through 12 are in.** Twelve parts, adapted from the source chapters.

Two rules shaped them:

**Battles only happen where the source has a fight.** Several chapters are
scenes end to end — no invented encounters were added to pad them out. A
scene-only chapter still pays its first-clear rewards and costs no stamina.

**Some fights aren't meant to be won.** Where the source has a fight that ends
in something other than victory, the battle **ends on its own once the enemy
drops to 20% health**, the scene takes over, and you're paid as normal. You're
not being asked to beat someone the story says you can't.

## Turns Play Out One Action at a Time

A real bug, and the fix changed how battles feel.

The engine used to resolve an entire turn instantly and then play the
animations back. For a moment before the first hit landed, the health bars
already showed the *end* of the turn — you could see the outcome before you saw
the fight.

Now each action resolves, plays, and only then hands over to the next one. What
you see is the actual state of the battle at that moment.

## The Hand Moves Now

Cards used to teleport. You picked one up and it reappeared somewhere else; a
merge deleted one card and quietly ranked up another; a new turn's hand arrived
already dealt and already merged.

Every one of those is animated now.

- **Press and hold a card** and everything it can merge with lights up. The
  rest of the hand dims, so what matters is what you can see
- **Drag** and the card follows your finger while the rest of the hand opens a
  space for it
- **Drag onto a lit card** and it flies into that card, which punches and
  ranks up
- **At the start of a turn**, cards deal in one at a time — and when two of
  them merge on arrival, you actually watch it happen

That last one was never a missing animation. The game merged those cards
*while dealing them*, so the pair that collided was never on the table to be
seen. Now the deal plays out properly.

All of it scales with your battle speed setting, and if you have reduced
motion enabled, cards simply appear where they belong.

## The Battle Screen

Rebuilt around a side rail. Both teams sit in one column with their health,
gauge and active effects always legible, and the log opens as a drawer instead
of eating the arena.

- **Uncancellable effects are hidden by default** — they're permanent, so
  showing them on every unit forever was noise. A toggle brings them back
- Card frames read their rank at a glance, and ultimates are unmistakable

## Events

The World Boss page is now the **events board**, and Molvarr is one entry on it
rather than the whole screen. Selecting a fight opens a briefing — the enemy's
statline, what's on the table, your difficulty setting and your team — before
you commit any stamina.

The two ascension trials are listed there too, locked, so the rank walls are no
longer something you hit with no explanation anywhere in the game.

## Summoning, Rebuilt

The whole flow: pulls, the reveal, and how milestones pay out.

- **5 gems for a single, 50 for a multi.** A multi is **11 pulls for the price
  of 10** — the eleventh is free
- **Banner progress moves 1:1 with gems spent** — 50 gems, 50 progress
- **500** hands you a **random featured unit from the banner**
- **1000** lets you **pick one**

The two milestones are independent. Claiming 1000 no longer wipes out an
unclaimed 500 — the bar only resets once you've reached the end **and** claimed
everything on the way. Rewards can't expire out from under you.

The Permanent banner still runs on tickets and is unchanged.

## Rules Clarified

**When the last enemy falls mid-turn**, only the cards that actually need an
enemy fizzle. Heals, cleanses and buffs still fire. An attacking ultimate isn't
spent — it **returns to the top of your next draw** and keeps its gauge.
Ultimates that aren't attacks go off as normal.

**Encounter difficulty now lives on the stage.** Some chapters give a side a
stat boost or an extra action, and the briefing lists them before you enter.
Most fights have none.

**Merging has one rule everywhere: same character, same skill, same rank.**
The Merge button used to be looser than that — it would feed a rank 1 card into
a rank 2 one — while merging by dragging never allowed it. Two rules for one
mechanic, and the highlight could only ever show one of them. The strict rule
won. A spare low-rank card can no longer be sacrificed into a higher one.

## Balance

A roster-wide pass on health and defence. Fights were ending in roughly **two
hits** on a focused character — fast enough that taunts, damage reduction,
heals and cleanses rarely got to matter.

- **Health roughly doubled** across the roster, into a 2,900–4,000 band
- **Defence up about 60%**
- **Attack is unchanged** — every skill multiplier is tuned against it

Skills that scale off health or defence were **brought down to compensate**, and
attack-scaled heals were raised, so nobody's damage or healing changed position
relative to anyone else's. Enemies were scaled to match.

The result is closer to four hits. Expect longer fights and more room for a
support kit to earn its slot.

## Starting Out

The game used to say nothing to a new player. You cleared the first chapter and
it went quiet, with a thousand gems in your wallet nobody had mentioned and a
stamina bar whose purpose was never stated.

**Bureau Orders** are a short list of starter objectives on the home screen.
Each one names somewhere to go, says why, and pays you for getting there —
clear a chapter, summon, raise someone to level 5, save a team, beat Molvarr,
ascend, reach rank 5. The list retires itself once you've claimed everything,
and a gold count on the TOLL wordmark tells you when something's waiting.

**Lyra joins you for good.** She has been fighting beside you on loan since
Part 2 began. Clear **Part 2, Chapter 2** and she stays — a permanent second
character, so the early stretch isn't fought with Duke alone. If you already
summoned her, you get an ultimate rank instead.

**Your first battle now explains itself.** Four short prompts cover the hand,
merging, how many actions you get, and the unit sitting on your bench. They
appear when the thing they describe is actually true, they never block a tap,
and doing the thing dismisses them. **Skip all** turns them off; *Show again*
under Account brings them back.

## A New Look

The interface has one colour language now, applied across the archive, news,
story, home, the navigation bar, your profile and the battle screen. Cyan is
reserved for the interface itself; the five element colours belong to
characters and nothing else.

Other things you'll notice:

- **Home** opens on where you left off, not a second copy of the menu
- **The archive** is where your roster lives, showing level, ascension and
  ultimate rank per character. Units you don't own are hidden until you ask
  for them
- **Your profile** carries account rank and progress, with inventory and
  account settings behind their own buttons
- **Locked story chapters are redacted rather than hidden** — you can see how
  much is ahead of you without the titles spoiling it

## Fixes

- **Cloud saves were only carrying half your account.** Account rank, world
  level, saved teams and your last team never left the device they were made
  on, so signing in elsewhere started you at rank 1 with no presets. All of it
  travels with the save now
- Description text corrected across a dozen kits: missing durations, effects
  naming the wrong target, "1 ultimate gauge(s)", and a heal that claimed to
  cleanse enemies
- The Growth window opening *behind* the page it was launched from
- A story boss rendering with no artwork at all
- Character pages showing base stats regardless of your investment
