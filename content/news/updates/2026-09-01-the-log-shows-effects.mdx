The battle log has always told you what an action *hit*: who took damage, how
much, what dodged, what died. It never told you what an action **did** beyond
that. A skill that lowered DEF by 30% for two turns showed up as a damage
number and nothing else, and the only way to find the rest was the raw text log
behind the Raw toggle.

No combat rules changed here. The log just stopped leaving things out.

## Every Status, On the Action That Caused It

Each action now lists the statuses it moved, under the targets it hit:

- **Green chips** are gains — a buff landing, a regeneration ticking on.
- **Red chips** are debuffs — a stat down, a seal, a damage-over-time with its
  per-turn number.
- **Struck-through chips** are losses — something cleansed, or an effect that
  stopped applying because the action ended it.

Durations come along too, so `DEF −30% · 2t` says the whole thing in one chip.

## Including the Ones That Were Never Targeted

This is the part the old log could never have shown. A lot of what happens in a
fight lands on somebody who was not the target of anything:

- a skill that buffs **its own user** after it swings
- a skill that buffs the **whole team** while striking one enemy
- an **Extort** link breaking on a third character because the enemy holding it
  died

All of those now appear in the log, named by whoever they happened to, instead
of quietly not being mentioned. If a fight swung and you could not tell why,
the reason is now written down.

## And When They Run Out

Effects ending is the other half. A stun wearing off changes no HP, so nothing
about it reached the log at all — the enemy simply acted again and you were
left to work out why. Expiries are now recorded on the turn they happen, struck
through, on whoever lost them.

Effects still counting down stay quiet. Only the turn something actually ends
gets a line.

## Refreshes Read as Refreshes

Recasting an effect that is already running does not read as "lost it, then got
it again" — it reads as the new duration, once. The log says what happened, not
what the bookkeeping looked like.

---

The Raw toggle is still there. It carries the engine's own commentary — resisted
and immune lines, passive narration, HP drains — which is prose rather than
state, and stays the fullest read of a fight.

## Elsewhere: Navigation Moved Down

On a phone the row of route icons at the top was 234px wide holding 332px of
routes, which meant two of them — News and Profile — were simply off the edge
of the screen, behind a sideways swipe nothing told you about.

Navigation is now **five tabs along the bottom**: Menu, Story, Events, Gacha
and You. All five are visible, all five are where your thumb already is, and
they get out of the way during a fight. Archive, Practice and News moved to the
main menu, which is what Menu opens.

The character archive got the same treatment from the other direction: search
and one **Filters** button, with elements, sorting, tags and mechanics behind
it. Characters used to start two thirds of the way down the screen. They start
near the top now.

Music volume moved to your profile, under **Sound**. The mute button stays in
the top bar on every screen — that is the one you want in a hurry, and it works
whether or not you are signed in.

On a desktop nothing has changed.
