A pass over the screens you actually spend time in. Nothing about combat
itself changed — every number, kit and ruling is exactly where it was.

## A Home Screen, Not a Menu

The main menu was seven identical buttons that told you nothing. It's now a
hub:

- **Your stamina, gems and coin** are visible the moment you land
- **MAIN STORY** shows the chapter you're actually up to next
- **WORLD BOSS** and **GACHA** show the current boss and the live banner
- Modes are sized by importance instead of all looking the same

The top navigation bar also now lists every mode. **World Boss, Gacha and
News were previously unreachable from any page except the home screen** —
that's fixed.

## You Can Inspect Enemies Now

Tap any enemy in battle to see their full details — stats, active effects,
ult gauge, complete kit. Previously this only worked for your own team.

Tapping a unit now always means "inspect", on both sides. Focus-firing an
enemy moved to its own ◎ button on the enemy's card, so the two actions can't
be confused. Marking a target is still optional — unmarked attacks pick a
living enemy at random, same as before.

## Rebuilt Character Info Panel

The in-battle info panel used to be one long scroll where you'd see about a
third of a character at a time. It's been rebuilt to fit on one screen:

- Stats sit over the character's artwork instead of beside a thumbnail
- HP, active effects and ult gauge come first — the things that change
- Skills moved into tabs, each showing that skill's own art
- Your team's roster and the enemy roster each have their own shortcut

HP numbers now show directly on the battle cards, so you don't have to open
anything to read them.

## Every Character Hits Differently

Impact effects used to be a generic coloured ring for all but five
characters. Every character now has their own: Killua and Seras crack with
lightning, Diane and Mustafa shake the ground, Siddiq blooms, Sara leaves
spectral paw marks, Meliodas trails demonic haze, Molvarr corrodes.

Ultimate cut-ins now show that ultimate's own artwork instead of the
character's portrait, so each one reads as its own move.

## Character Pages Read Like Pages

Archive entries were a stack of boxes inside boxes. They're now laid out as
documents, matching these patch notes:

- Each skill gets a heading and a **rank table** — R1/R2/R3 side by side with
  their damage multipliers, so you can see exactly what a merge buys you
- Skill type and mechanics are listed under the name
- Passives and the damage preview follow the same layout
- Multi-phase bosses get the same treatment, per phase

## Growth Moved

The level and ascension controls used to sit permanently expanded on every
character's archive page — including characters you don't own and enemies you
never will. They're now behind a **Growth** button that only appears for
characters you actually own.

## Fixes

- The Preview training dummy no longer dies partway through — it now has
  enough health to survive anything you throw at it, so you can try a
  character's full skill set and ultimate in one session
- The battle log is now grouped by turn and collapsible, showing exactly what
  hit what for how much, with crits, dodges and knockouts marked
- Ability descriptions, damage previews and every combat rule are unchanged
