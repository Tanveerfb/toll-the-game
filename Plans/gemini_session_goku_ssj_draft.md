# Super Saiyan Goku (Namek Saga) - Collab
**Role**: Defense (Duelist)
**Color**: Light
**Tags**: Collab, Saiyan, Dragon Ball Z

## Design Philosophy
SSJ Goku and Final Form Frieza are **next-tier power**. They sit above the current roster ceiling.
- **SSJ Goku** is the defensive half of the pair: he outlasts, he endures, and he hits back harder the more his team suffers. He doesn't need to kill you fast — he needs to still be standing when you've spent everything.
- **Frieza** is the offensive half: he peaks on turn 1 and decays, the opposite clock.

## Suggested Stats
* **HP**: 4000 (Ties Yalina for the highest HP in the game. He's meant to survive.)
* **ATK**: 200 (Respectable but not dominant. His damage comes from passive multipliers stacking, not raw ATK.)
* **DEF**: 210 (Defense-role tier. Combined with his passive damage reduction, he's extremely hard to kill.)

*(Notes: These stats place him firmly in the Defense band [110-175 ATK, 210-240 DEF, 3600-4000 HP], but his ATK is pushed above the band ceiling to 200 because he IS next-tier power and still needs to deal respectable damage. His passive multipliers do the rest.)*

## Passive: Super Saiyan
```markdown
# During battle
- Damage reduction 10% 👆

# While an ally is defeated
- ATK 50% 👆 (Uncancellable)

# When facing only 1 enemy on the field
- Damage dealt 30% 👆
- Damage reduction 25% 👆
```
*(Notes: This passive is what makes him "next tier" despite moderate base ATK. At 200 ATK with the ally-down buff, he effectively has 300 ATK — and when he corners the last enemy, his damage jumps another 30% on top of that. Meanwhile, his 10% baseline DR stacks to 35% DR against a solo enemy, making him nearly unkillable.)*

## Skills

### Skill 1: Kamehameha
**Description:** Does [Concentrate] damage equal to 230/280/350% ATK to one enemy.
*(Notes: Same Kamehameha as his Saiyan Saga form. [Concentrate] synergizes perfectly with his "1 enemy on field" passive — when he's cornered the last enemy, he gets +30% from passive AND +50% from Concentrate, stacking into a devastating finisher.)*

### Skill 2: Meteor Combination
**Description:** Raises ATK and greatly raises DEF for 1 turn and then does damage equal to 195/240/300% ATK to one enemy.
*(Notes: No mechanic — the skill's value is the buff package. Multipliers bumped to 195/240/300% so the damage is respectable on its own.)*

### Ultimate: Angry Kamehameha
**Description:** Does [Desperation] damage equal to 600% ATK to one enemy.
*(Notes: [Desperation] is a proposed new mechanic — damage increases by 1.2% per 1% HP missing (uncapped). At 10% HP with an ally down: 300 ATK × 6.0 × 2.08 [Desperation] × 1.3 [passive] - 160 DEF = ~4,534 damage. Still a one-shot on almost anything, just not as absurdly overkill.)*
