# Final Form Frieza (Full Power) - Collab
**Role**: Damage Dealer (Executioner)
**Color**: Dark (opposite SSJ Goku's Light — mutual type advantage both ways)
**Tags**: Collab, Frieza Force, Dragon Ball Z

## Design Philosophy
SSJ Goku and Final Form Frieza are **next-tier power**. They sit above the current roster ceiling.
- **Frieza** is the offensive half: he opens at maximum power and decays every turn. Two clocks running opposite directions — stall him and you win; feed him kills and it snowballs.
- **SSJ Goku** is the defensive half: he outlasts and hits harder the more desperate things get.

**This is the 100% Full Power version** — the one Frieza cannot sustain. A kit that opens at maximum and slides every turn IS that form.

## Suggested Stats
* **HP**: 3200 (Mid-range DPS band. He doesn't need to survive long — he needs to kill before the decay catches him.)
* **ATK**: 300 (Ties Tao for the highest base ATK in the game. His +50% passive pushes him to an effective **450 ATK** on turn 1 — that IS the "next tier".)
* **DEF**: 105 (Low-mid DPS band. He's all offense.)

## Passive: Emperor of the Universe
```markdown
# At the start of battle
- Damage reduction 50% 👆 (Uncancellable)
- ATK and DEF 50% 👆 (Uncancellable)

# At the start of each turn, starting from turn 2
- Damage reduction 10% 👇 (up to a maximum of 40%)
- ATK and DEF 10% 👇 (up to a maximum of 40%)

# When this unit defeats an enemy
- ATK 15% 👆 (Uncancellable)
- Damage reduction 5% 👆 (Uncancellable) (max 6 times)
```
*(Notes: Turn 1 he's a god — 450 effective ATK, 157 effective DEF, 50% DR. By turn 4 he's down to 20% of each bonus [the floor]. Each kill buys back exactly one turn of decay. The kill bonus needs no "Max N" because the enemy team is finite — 4 units is the natural cap.)*

## Skills

### Skill 1: Death Beam
**Description:** Does [Execution] damage equal to 220/250/300% ATK to one enemy.
*(Notes: [Execution] is a proposed new mechanic — defeats a target left below 10% HP after the hit.  This is Frieza's signature: precise, lethal, and efficient at cleaning up weakened targets.)*

### Skill 2: Death Saucer
**Description:** Does damage equal to 160/200/250% ATK to one enemy and applies [Bleed] for 2 turns; consumes 10% of current HP. (HP cannot go below 1).
*(Notes: The self-damage is the joke from the source material — his own disc is what cuts him in half. [Bleed] is a DoT equal to 90% of the damage dealt, so at his turn 1 ATK of 450, this applies a devastating DoT on top of the hit.)*

### Ultimate: Supernova
**Description:** Does damage equal to 500% ATK to all enemies.
*(Notes: No mechanic — pure, raw, overwhelming power. At turn 1 with 450 effective ATK: 450 × 5.0 = 2,250 base damage to EVERY enemy. That's enough to nearly one-shot most DPS units through their DEF on the very first ultimate. The lack of a mechanic is the point — Frieza doesn't need tricks, he just destroys everything.)*
