## Suggested Stats
* **HP**: 3300 (Brought up from 1230. Since he sits for 3 turns with 70% damage reduction, 3300 HP makes him durable enough to survive the setup phase without being unkillable.)
* **ATK**: 287 (Preserves "Head of the Exam Commission for the 287th Hunter Exam"; sits near the absolute top of the 190-300 DPS band, fitting for Netero.)
* **DEF**: 110 (Preserves "Least confirmed age at the time of death"; fits perfectly into the 80-160 DPS band.)

## Passive: Bodhisattva's Vow
```markdown
# At the start of battle
- Applies [Suppressed] to self for 3 turns. (Uncancellable)

# While this character has [Suppressed]
- Damage reduction 70% 👆
- Cannot attack and cannot gain ultimate gauge

# When [Suppressed] effect is removed
- Gains [Pinnacle of Nen Mastery] for the duration of the battle. (Uncancellable)

# While this character has [Pinnacle of Nen Mastery]
- When using a skill: Deals a follow-up attack equal to 30% of the damage dealt by the skill. (Max 1 per skill use; Same target; Skipped if target is defeated)
- Immune to all stat decrease effects
- When facing only 1 enemy on the field: Damage dealt 50% 👆, and type advantage is neutralized when taking damage
```
*(Notes: As per rulings, he still draws cards while Suppressed, they are just unplayable. Stat decrease immunity is not full Debuff Immunity. Follow-up does not count as an attack for attack counters like Full Counter. Type-neutral is defensive only.)*

## Skills

### Skill 2: Ninety-Ninth Hand
**Description:** Does [Rupture] damage equal to 165/200/250% ATK to all enemies.
*(Notes: NERFED from your draft's 195/240/300%. Why? Because [Rupture] deals 2x damage against buffed enemies, and Netero gets a free 30% follow-up attack on top of it. 300% base AoE with Rupture AND a follow-up is too extreme. 165/200/250% matches the standard AoE damage band like Batra's.)*

### Ultimate: Zero Hand
**Description:** Does [Power Strike] damage equal to 500% ATK to one enemy and increases ultimate gauge by 2.
*(Notes: Multiplier untouched. Between [Power Strike] and the +50% single-enemy passive, 500% base makes this one of the hardest-hitting ultimates in the game, which is exactly how Zero Hand should feel.)*
