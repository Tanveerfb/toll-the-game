# Character headings — a proposal for every unit

**Date:** 2026-09-17 · **Status:** **23 of 31 approved**, 2 in workshop, 6 open
(the generics) · **Implements:** ruling **#141**

Tanveer, 2026-09-17, delegating the drafting:

> *"Heading text — not just Lyra, for everyone actually. I can't do it right now,
> and I think I will ask you to suggest headings for me for each of the
> characters, not just Lyra, not just NPC versions — everyone. Now, NPC ones
> won't have a fancy heading text. And even the current playable cast, they
> don't need to have a fancy one — just something that will differentiate them
> from their future potential other instances or new versions of them."*

**Names are his** (`AGENTS.md`, ruling #65). This is drafting on request.

---

## What a heading is — corrected 2026-09-17

**A heading is a title or a nickname. It is not a compressed lore line.** His
correction, on the first draft's *"Nine Years Alone"* for Duke:

> *"What do you mean by Nine Years Alone for Duke? We don't need to have their
> lore inside. It could be like 'a water-based martial artist' or something like
> that. It's more like a title, a nickname — not a lore heading."*

The first draft got the **purpose** right and the **register** wrong. It was
written as *"the one true sentence about this character's situation"*, which
produces biography: *Nine Years Alone*, *Phase One Examiner*, *Fairy Hybrid*.
Every one of those is a **fact about the character** rather than a **name for
this version of them**, and he rejected them in a block.

So the test a heading has to pass is now two-part:

1. **Does it read as something you would call the character?** A nickname, a
   fighting style, a rank, a role — the words a herald or a rival would use.
   *Red Ice Archer*, *Lion of Khalsa*, *Heir of the Zoldyck Clan*. Not a
   summary of where they have been.
2. **Could the strongest future version also wear it?** If yes, it is still
   wrong — it spends a label the future needs. This half survived the
   correction, because it is the job he gave the field: *"differentiate them
   from their future potential other instances or new versions of them."*

**Generic fails the first test as hard as biography does.** *Momentum* for
Yalina and *Phase One Qualifier* for the four unnamed candidates were both cut
for being *"way too generic"* — a heading nobody would actually call them is not
a nickname, it is a label.

## In workshop — 2

### Duke

Rejected: *"Nine Years Alone"* (biography), then *"Flowing Ruin"* alone — which
he called good but incomplete. His brief:

> *"Flowing Ruin is a good one. Master of the Flowing Ruin could work as well.
> Or something that can show that he's not yet that comfortable — he's not a
> master of his art yet, but he is… he's the one who made it."*

**Duke invented Flowing Ruin and has not mastered it.** That is the state the
heading has to hold — and note that **he named the future card himself**:
*Master of the Flowing Ruin* is what a later Duke wears, which is exactly why
this one cannot.

⚠️ **A question his brief raises and does not answer: Seras is playable.**
Her heading is visible from the day she is summoned, which is long before the
story reveal. Menace in the heading tells the player she is a villain early.

That may be exactly right — plenty of games telegraph it, and he asked for the
presence explicitly. But if the reveal should land cold, the menace belongs on a
**second Seras card released after it**, and this one stays neutral. **His
call**; the field supports either, since a later version takes a different
heading by design.

## Generic enemies — settled: **Common Foe**

Six units are **types, not individuals**: `checkpoint_bruiser`,
`checkpoint_enforcer`, `ford_bandit`, `road_bandit`, `raider`, `wild_beast`.

He asked for a heading on them anyway: *"just have a generic heading for them,
for the enemies."* All six share **Common Foe**, which says what they are
without pretending they are somebody.

**The faction set offered first — Checkpoint / Bandit / Raider / Wilds — was
dropped on inspection**, because a faction heading stutters against the name it
sits above: *Raider* over **Raider**, *Bandit* over **Ford Bandit**, *Checkpoint*
over **Checkpoint Bruiser**. Three of the six read as a repeat. A shared generic
heading over six differing names does not, and heading + name stays unique
either way.

**The exempt list in `tests/characterHeadings.test.ts` is gone.** Every kit now
carries a heading, so the guard has no exceptions — and no list for a new kit to
hide in.

## Built, 2026-09-17

- **`heading?: string`** on `characterSchema.ts` and `CharacterData`. Optional,
  display-only; nothing in `combat.ts` reads it.
- **The 23 approved headings are in `data/characters/*.json`** — one inserted
  line per kit, no reformatting.
- **`tests/characterHeadings.test.ts`** pins four things: heading + name
  identify one unit together (the check that would have caught the duplicate
  "Lyra" the day it shipped), a heading stays title-length, no named character
  is left without one, and the roster is actually found. The uniqueness guard
  was **falsified** — removing `lyra_npc`'s heading turns it red.
- `duke` and `seras` are exempt in that guard **only until he picks**. Removing
  them from the exempt list is part of the same commit as their headings.

## Built: display and URLs, 2026-09-17 (ruling #143)

- **Archive entry pages show the heading above the name**, and print
  `No. <cardNumber>` where they used to print `id`. Battle UI is untouched —
  *"headings wouldn't appear in battle UI. But on archive entry pages? Yes."*
- **`/archive/[id]` → `/archive/character/[cardNumber]`.** His rule: *"the url
  would show the char id. Not the names."* The catch was that `id` **is** a name
  (`duke`, `batra`) and is also the key every save's `roster` holds, so it could
  not be renumbered. `cardNumber` is a second immutable identifier, 100001-100031,
  assigned once and never reused.
- `archiveHref()` is the only place that path is spelled.

## Still undecided: the other display surfaces

`TeamPicker`, `CharacterBrowser` and the gacha reveal still show a bare name.
Neither Lyra collides in those lists today (one is `storyOnly`, so they sit in
different archives), so nothing is broken — but they are the other places a
player compares two units. **His call whether headings go there too.**

## Superseded: the display recommendation

The field exists and nothing renders it yet, so the duplicate "Lyra" is still
visible in game.

**This needs one decision from him, because it is placement** (#139 — UI is
his). A name renders in **23 places**, and they are not all the same kind of
surface: an archive page has room for a title above a name, a **47px hand card
does not**, and battle density is explicitly his (`AGENTS.md`).

**Recommendation — a shared `CharacterName` component used in the roomy
surfaces only:** archive list and detail, the NPC archive, `TeamPicker`,
`CharacterBrowser`, the gacha reveal and the event brief. **Battle stays
untouched** — tiles, cards and the log keep the bare name.

That covers every surface where a player *compares* two units, which is the
only place the heading has a job to do.
