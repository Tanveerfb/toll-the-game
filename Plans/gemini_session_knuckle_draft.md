# Knuckle Bine (Collab - Standard)
**Role**: Damage Dealer
**Color**: (To be decided)
**Tags**: Collab, Human, Hunter x Hunter

## Suggested Stats
* **HP**: 3500 (Brought up to the current Damage Dealer band of 2900-3600 to help him survive [APR]'s forced aggro)
* **ATK**: 200 (Preserves Manga Chapter debut reference; fits the 190-300 band)
* **DEF**: 86 (Preserves Anime Episode debut reference; fits the 80-160 band and makes taking hits risky)

## Passive: Hakoware
```markdown
# When dealing damage to an enemy with a skill
- Applies [APR] to the target with an initial value equal to the damage dealt. (Only 1 [APR] may exist; Uncancellable)

# While an enemy is affected by [APR]
- Target's basic stats 20% 👇
- Target's single-target attacks are taunted towards this character (Ignores other taunts)

# At the end of every turn
- [APR] value increases by 10%

# When dealing damage to the [APR] target with a skill
- [APR] value increases by 50% of the damage dealt

# When taking damage from the [APR] target
- [APR] value decreases by the damage taken. (If it reaches 0, [APR] is removed)

# When [APR] value exceeds the target's max HP
- Converts [APR] to [IRS], defeating the target at the end of the turn
```
*(Notes: As per rulings, [APR] is one single uncancellable effect. Knuckle's taunt is high priority but only for the [APR] target. If Knuckle dies, [APR] disappears.)*

## Skills

### Skill 1: Bankrupt Punch
**Description:** Does damage equal to 325/400/500% ATK to one enemy; damage increases by 20% if the target has [APR].
*(Notes: The 20% bonus stays flat across all ranks. 325% is still the highest R1 damage on the roster, which is necessary to get the [APR] debt snowballing, but the 500% R3 cap prevents it from being too oppressive.)*

### Skill 2: Interest Accrual
**Description:** Does [Detonate] damage equal to 325/400/500% ATK to one enemy.

### Ultimate: Chapter 7 Bankruptcy
**Description:** Raises ATK and DEF for 3 turns, and then does damage equal to 500% ATK to one enemy.
*(Notes: "Raises" means exactly 30%. Because it comes before the damage clause, it boosts this ultimate's own damage per ruling #22.)*
