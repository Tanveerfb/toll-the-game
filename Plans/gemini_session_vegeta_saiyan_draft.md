## Skills

### Skill 1: Galick Gun
**Description:** Does [Detonate] damage equal to 230/280/350% ATK to one enemy.
*(Notes: Changed from [Pierce] to [Detonate]. We don't currently have a literal "anti-concentrate" mechanic in the engine that scales with MORE enemies, but [Detonate] (which deals +20% damage per point of the enemy's ultimate gauge) serves as a great foil to Goku!)*

### Skill 2: Meteor Crash
**Description:** Does damage equal to 195/240/300% ATK to one enemy and disables Ultimate Moves for 1/1/2 turns.
*(Notes: Renamed to Meteor Crash to give him a canonical melee rush attack! I kept the "disables ultimates" utility because it fits the idea of him pummeling the enemy so hard they can't focus on their ultimate.)*

### Ultimate: Super Galick Gun
**Description:** Does [Detonate] damage equal to 500% ATK to one enemy.
*(Notes: Changed to [Detonate] to match Skill 1, giving him a massive nuke against enemies who are hoarding their ultimate gauge!)*
